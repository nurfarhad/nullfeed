## Detailed Store Description (Copy & Paste into Chrome Web Store Developer Dashboard)

**Reclaim your time and attention. Eliminate endless algorithmic feeds, shorts, reels, and distractions on desktop.**

Nullfeed is a hyper-lightweight, privacy-first Chrome extension designed to strip away the addictive hooks engineered to trap you in doomscroll loops. Transform distracting social sites into intentional, calm tools.

---

### ✨ Key Features

#### 🎬 YouTube — Watch Intentionally
* **Hide YouTube Shorts**: Removes Shorts shelves, navigation tabs, chip links, and lockups from your home feed and sidebar.
* **Instant Shorts URL Redirect**: Opening direct `/shorts/...` links automatically takes you to clean standard viewing.
* **Hide Recommended Sidebar**: Collapses the "Up Next / Recommended Videos" panel on watch pages to stop endless video rabbit holes.
* **Calm Home Feed**: Filters intrusive algorithmic watch-history recommendations, replacing endless feeds with intentional search or calm explore browsing.
* **Hide Comments**: Eliminates comment section rabbit holes and arguments.
* **Always-On End Screen Blocking**: Prevents intrusive pop-up video overlays and countdown autoplays as videos end.

#### 📘 Facebook — Communicate Without The Trap
* **Direct to Messages**: Automatically routes root Facebook visits straight to Messenger (`/messages/`) so you can stay in touch with friends without seeing the news feed.
* **Hide Reels**: Completely strips Reels trays, video reels, and reels navigation.
* **Hide Stories**: Collapses empty story carousels with clean zero-gap layout.
* **Hide Videos & Auto-Pause**: Hides feed video units and automatically pauses background video playback.
* **Ad & Sponsored Post Blocker**: Silently cleans sponsored clutter and right-rail ad units.

#### 📸 Instagram — Direct & Intentional
* **Direct to Messages**: Directly routes root Instagram visits to Direct Messages (`/direct/inbox/`) so you never get sucked into the algorithmic feed.
* **Hide Reels**: Removes Reels from the sidebar navigation and profile pages.
* **Hide Stories & Highlights**: Removes top story trays and profile story highlights with clean layout collapse.
* **Hide Explore**: Strips the Explore icon and algorithmic discovery grid.

#### 🧘 Mindful Moments & Focus Cycle
* **Mindful Quote Card**: Replaces empty feed spaces with inspiring philosophical and mindful thoughts from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Naval Ravikant, Steve Jobs, and more.
* **Intent Prompt**: Set an intentional goal for your session ("What did you come here to do?") that stays pinned as a gentle anchor.
* **Smart Trigger**: Detects compulsive fast-scrolling patterns and gently re-engages protection to prevent mindless scrolling.
* **Multi-Platform Support**: Enjoy serene feed replacement on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Reddit.

#### ⏱️ Productivity Controls
* **Snooze Protection**: Need a quick 5 or 15-minute break? Use the quick snooze pill with a live countdown timer and instant one-click resume.
* **Master Switch**: Pause or resume all protections globally with a single click.

---

### 🔒 100% Private. Zero Telemetry.
* **No Tracking**: No analytics, no metrics collection, no trackers, and no third-party scripts.
* **Zero External Calls**: Nullfeed never communicates with external servers or APIs. All code runs 100% locally in your browser.
* **No Account Required**: Free forever with zero sign-ups or paywalls.
* **Ultra Lightweight**: Built with Preact and native CSS tokens for sub-millisecond execution and zero battery drain.

---

### 🛡️ Permissions Transparency
Nullfeed requests only the absolute minimum permissions required to function:
* **`storage`**: Saves your toggle preferences locally on your machine via Chrome Storage API.
* **Host Permissions**: Enables Nullfeed to apply styling and clean DOM elements on supported domains (`facebook.com`, `instagram.com`, `youtube.com`, `linkedin.com`, `x.com`, `reddit.com`).
