Nullfeed - Eradicate Distractions & Focus


[2] SUMMARY / SHORT DESCRIPTION (Under 132 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eliminate addictive feeds, shorts, reels, and ads on YouTube, Facebook & Instagram. Replace voids with mindful focus quotes.


[3] DETAILED DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Take back control of your focus, time, and mental clarity.

Nullfeed is a 100% free, privacy-first Chrome extension that eradicates addictive short-form video feeds, algorithmic rabbit holes, and distracting advertisements across your favorite web platforms — replacing the endless feed void with mindful focus quotes.

Use social media for its true purpose — messaging, intentional search, and productive connection — without getting trapped in endless algorithmic scrolling loops.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🚀 WHAT'S NEW IN VERSION 3.0.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 🌓 Automatic Dark & Light Theme: Automatically senses and adapts to your device or Chrome system appearance with an elegant, physical UI8 Bento Pro minimalist design. Zero configuration required.
• 🎨 Sleek Feature Icons: Refined, minimalist Tabler icons on every platform toggle for quick, intuitive navigation.
• 🔄 Automatic Focus Cycle: Fixed 15m-blocked / 15m-open interval across feeds on Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Reddit, replacing infinite scrolling with mindful focus quotes.
• 🛡️ Enhanced Ad & Sponsored Blocker: Immediate, zero-flicker suppression of sponsored feed posts and commercial sidebar rails.
• ⚡ Blazing Fast Manifest V3 Engine: Pre-emptive CSS injection eliminates screen flickering and layout shifting completely.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
✨ KEY FEATURES & PLATFORM SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🎯 1. MINDFUL FOCUS QUOTES
• Replaces empty feed spaces with a sleek, serene focus card.
• Curated quotes on focus, stoicism, and deep work from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Naval Ravikant, Steve Jobs, and more.
• Interactive quote cycling to refresh your inspiration anytime.
• Seamlessly styled for both Dark and Light modes.

📺 2. YOUTUBE
• Hide Shorts: Eradicates Shorts shelves, grids, and recommendation carousels on Home, Subscriptions, and Search feeds.
• Clean Navigation: Removes the Shorts button from the left sidebar and mini-guide.
• Auto-Redirect: Directly navigates away from distracting /shorts/... URLs back to your clean home feed.
• Hide Recommended Sidebar: Blocks the "Up Next / Related Videos" sidebar on watch pages to stop endless video chains.

👥 3. FACEBOOK
• Hide Reels: Removes Reels carousels, feed cards, and video suggestions.
• Hide Stories: Collapses story trays with zero layout gaps or empty placeholders.
• Hide Videos & Auto-Pause: Blocks distracting video posts and pauses background autoplay.
• Ad & Sponsored Blocker: Cleans away sponsored ads, feed promotions, and commercial side rails immediately.

📸 4. INSTAGRAM
• Hide Reels: Removes the Reels icon from the sidebar, removes the Reels tab on profiles, and redirects /reels/ pages.
• Hide Stories & Highlights: Hides story trays and profile story highlights smoothly without layout gaps.
• Clean Explore: Hides the Explore button to prevent doomscrolling while leaving Messages, Search, and Profile posts completely intact.

🔄 5. AUTOMATIC FOCUS CYCLE & CONTROLS
• 15m Blocked / 15m Open: Runs an automated, non-intrusive focus cycle on feeds across Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and Reddit.
• Serene Quote Card: Replaces endless scrolling with a compact, inspiring focus quote card during the focus window.
• Master Protection Toggle: Pause or re-enable the entire extension with a single click.
